Prepared for Malik; no message has been sent. This is a request outline, not a claim of partnership or integration approval.

ELPIDA is researching stock-token quote pairing on Ethereum, BNB and Solana. All live stock capabilities are disabled. Its first transfer/graduation rehearsal used canonical Ethereum NVDAon; the Ethereum directory also lists TSLAon/SPYon feed variants. We need exact product semantics before selecting an oracle or presenting an eligible launch option.

Ask Ondo and/or Chainlink's data-feeds team to confirm:

1. The supported native-chain NVDAon total-return reference, if available, and its exact contract/feed identifier. Does the absence of NVDAon from our downloaded Ethereum catalog reflect missing coverage or another discovery route?
2. Which TSLAon and SPYon Ethereum primary proxy should a protocol consume? The catalog has API-labelled and Calculated/Kalman Shared-SVR variants. Explain the source composition, intended use and deprecation status of each.
3. Is each answer already USD per whole raw onchain token, with dividend/split/share multipliers included? Supply exact implementation/source and methodology references; specify whether any second multiplier is ever needed.
4. What source exposes current and scheduled corporate-action pauses, effective time, resumption confirmation, stale/frozen states and any trading halts? Provide the canonical ABI/schema and chain/address bindings. A recent price round alone cannot clear these states.
5. How do source-observation, transmission, primary/secondary proxy, cutoff-time and market-session timestamps differ? What heartbeat/deviation, closed-market behavior and maximum-age policies are appropriate? Does a feed carry explicit status or merely hold its last price?
6. What integration/usage permission, contract-custody and secondary-market eligibility rules apply to ELPIDA curves, routers, LP lockers, fee recipients and users in the intended jurisdictions? Primary API onboarding and general transferability are separate questions.
