How Elpida works

  1. Create a token

    Every token has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000. On enabled networks, you pay a $10 launch fee converted by a verified on-chain USD feed, or 10 USDC where supported and make the first purchase in the same transaction. There is no liquidity deposit.

  2. Trade on the bonding curve

    The price comes from a virtual bonding curve. It is not a funded pool at launch: purchases build a trading reserve, and sales are paid from that reserve. Each trade pays a 1.25% fee, split equally between Elpida and the creator.

  3. Graduate to a DEX

    When the curve sells out, its trading reserve and the remaining tokens open a DEX pool at the final curve price: Uniswap v3 on Base Sepolia and the local DEX simulation. Other EVM testnets use a locked test pool. Existing Solana tokens use PumpSwap. That liquidity is locked permanently.

  4. Earn creator fees

    Creator fees follow the reward settings chosen at launch: creator wallets, holder distributions, a reward-token purchase, a buyback with vesting, or allocations to wallets, X accounts and websites.

Elpida currently runs on a local simulation and public testnets only. No mainnet launch is available.
