How Elpida works

  1. Create a token

    Every token has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000. You pay a 10 test USDC creation fee and make the first purchase in the same transaction. There is no liquidity deposit.

  2. Trade on the bonding curve

    The price comes from a virtual bonding curve. It is not a funded pool at launch: purchases build a trading reserve, and sales are paid from that reserve. Each trade pays a 1.25% fee, split equally between Elpida and the creator.

  3. Graduate to a DEX

    When the curve sells out, its trading reserve and the remaining tokens open a DEX pool at the final curve price: Uniswap v3 on EVM networks, PumpSwap on Solana. That liquidity is locked permanently.

  4. Earn creator fees

    Creator fees follow the reward settings chosen at launch: creator wallets, holder distributions, a reward-token purchase, a buyback with vesting, or allocations to wallets, X accounts and websites.

Elpida currently runs on a local simulation and public testnets only. No mainnet launch is available.
